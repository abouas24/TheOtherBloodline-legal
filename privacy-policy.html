Privacy Policy

The Other Bloodline · version 2026-08-18

The short version

The Other Bloodline keeps your game on your device. We do not issue accounts, we hold no password, and we store no copy of your save. We do not know who you are, and we do not want to.

You may optionally link your Google Play Games account so your progress survives a lost or replaced phone. That is Google's account, not one we create, and the backup is held by Google under it — we never receive your save.

Three third parties do receive some data when you play: Google AdMob (if you watch a rewarded ad), RevenueCat and Google Play (if you make a purchase), and our own gameplay analytics. Each is described below, and the analytics can be switched off.

What is stored on your device

Your warband, progress, settings and chosen player name are saved in your device's local storage. They never leave your device unless you export the save yourself.

Your player name is visible only to you. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Deleting the app, or using Reset Save in Settings, removes this data permanently. We hold no copy, so we cannot restore it.

Advertising

Rewarded video ads are supplied by Google AdMob and are always optional — you choose to watch one in exchange for a reward. We never show ads you did not ask for.

To serve them, AdMob may process your device's advertising identifier, approximate location derived from IP address, and basic app-interaction data. That processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before any personalised advertising. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time from Settings, under Privacy.

If you are in a US state with an applicable privacy law, you can limit the sale or sharing of your data from the same Settings screen.

Cloud save and Google Play Games

Linking your Google Play Games account is optional. The game plays identically without it; the only thing it changes is whether your progress can be recovered on a new device.

If you link it, your save file is stored by Google Play Games under your own Google account, using Google's Saved Games feature. We do not host it, we cannot read it, and we keep no copy. Google's handling of it is governed by their privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

While linked, the game can see your Play Games display name and player ID so it can show you which account is connected. Neither is sent to us or to anyone else, and neither is stored anywhere but your device.

You can unlink at any time from your device's Google Play Games settings. Deleting the cloud copy is done from the same place, since it is your Google account that holds it.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

RevenueCat processes purchase and entitlement records on our behalf so the app knows what you own. Their privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Analytics

We collect gameplay events — for example that a stage was cleared or a screen was opened — to understand where the game is confusing or unfair.

Each event carries a random install identifier. It is generated on your device the first time you play, it is not linked to your name, your email, your Google account or your advertising identifier, and it lets us tell "one player returned five times" from "five players played once". It does not identify you as a person, and it is destroyed when you reset your save or uninstall the app.

Events also carry no name, no email, no contact details and no advertising identifier.

You can switch analytics off entirely in Settings, under Privacy. When it is off, nothing is sent and the identifier is discarded. The game plays identically either way.

Children

The Other Bloodline is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

Switch analytics off, or change your advertising consent, in Settings under Privacy.

Delete everything with Reset Save in Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Ask us anything, or request deletion of anything you believe we hold, at ToBsupport@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the app will tell you the next time you open it. The version of this policy is shown at the bottom of this screen.
