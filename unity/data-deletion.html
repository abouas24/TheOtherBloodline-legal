Deleting your data

This page explains how to delete the data associated with The Other Bloodline, published by Alexander Bouas.

You do not need the app installed to use anything on this page.

If you would rather just ask, email ToBsupport@gmail.com and we will handle it.

Delete your cloud save account

If you turned on cloud save, we hold copies of your save on our server — the latest one and older copies we keep to check saves and keep the Arena fair — under the player ID Google gave us when you signed in with Play Games. Deleting them deletes your account with us: the saves, the sync history, the cheat-check notes, your purchase records, your Arena record, your syndicate membership and the name it showed, your posts, reactions and any notice you set, your feud records and your support code. It is immediate and permanent. A syndicate you lead passes to its highest-ranked, longest-serving member, or is disbanded if you were its last.

In the app: Settings, then Account, then Delete my cloud save, and confirm. Your progress on that phone is not touched, and cloud save is switched off on it.

Without the app: email ToBsupport@gmail.com with your support code (shown in Settings under Account, in the form XXXX-XXXX), the Play Games name you play under, the deepest stage you reached, and the names of two creatures in your warband. We ask for those because the code alone does not prove the account is yours. We will only reply to the address you write from, and we will complete the deletion within 30 days, usually within a few days.

After a deletion we keep one thing: a record that the player ID was deleted, and when, and — only if the Arena ladder was closed to it at that moment or in the 30 days before — the date it was last closed, or the deletion date if it was closed then. It contains nothing else. It stops an old copy of the game from putting the deleted save back, and stops deleting from being used to get back onto the Arena ladder.

Signing in again afterwards does not bring cloud save back by itself: the game says your cloud save was deleted, and uploads nothing until you tap Start cloud save again.

Cancel any subscription first

If you have an active subscription, cancel it before deleting anything. Deleting your save does NOT cancel a subscription, and you would keep being billed for something you can no longer use.

Cancel it in the Google Play Store app: tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions.

If you are unsure whether you have one, that screen is also where you find out.

Delete the data on your device

Uninstall the app. That removes your warband, progress, settings, player name and analytics identifier from the device, immediately and permanently.

Uninstalling does not delete the copies on our server. Delete those from the section above.

The analytics data

If you left analytics on, we hold gameplay events — that a stage was cleared, that a screen was opened. They carry no name, no email, no contact details and no advertising identifier.

They are keyed by a random install identifier generated on your device. This version of the game does not display it, and we link it to nothing else, so we cannot find your rows from anything you could send us — which is also why those rows do not identify you as a person.

To stop them at once, switch analytics off in Settings, under Privacy: nothing more is sent, and the identifier on your device is discarded. Uninstalling does the same.

The analytics identifier is never stored with your cloud save, and your player ID is never sent with analytics, so neither can be used to find the other.

You can still email ToBsupport@gmail.com with any question about this data, and we will answer within 30 days.

What we cannot delete, and why

Purchase and refund records. If you bought something, Google Play holds a record of the transaction for tax, accounting, fraud-prevention and regulatory reasons, and neither we nor you can erase it. It is not deleted by any step above.

Data held by Google. Your Play Games profile, and what Google Play Games records about how you use it, are governed by Google and controlled from your Google account, not by us.

Data held by the ad companies. What Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads and Google AdMob process to show and measure an ad is held by them under their own policies (unity.com/legal/privacy-policy and policies.google.com/privacy), not by us; we never receive it. Resetting or deleting your advertising identifier in Android's settings breaks its link to your device.

The record of a deletion. After a deletion we keep one thing: a record that the player ID was deleted, and when, and — only if the Arena ladder was closed to it at that moment or in the 30 days before — the date it was last closed, or the deletion date if it was closed then. It contains nothing else. It stops an old copy of the game from putting the deleted save back, and stops deleting from being used to get back onto the Arena ladder.

Aggregate counts. Numbers that no longer refer to any individual — how many players cleared a stage in a given week — remain, because there is nothing in them to delete.
