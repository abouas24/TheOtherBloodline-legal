Your player name

The game gives you a name, and you can choose another in Settings. Names that are abusive, hateful, sexually explicit, impersonate us or our staff, or break the law are not permitted, and the game will reject them.

If you found or join a syndicate held on our server, its members see your name. The game tells you so, naming the name, before you found or join one, and founding or joining is your agreement to it. You can change your name at any time in Settings. If you ask to join a listed Syndicate, its leaders see your name until your request is answered, withdrawn or expires. Your name is shown beside what you say in chat, to the players in that room. If you choose to show your name on the Richest Hoards board, every player who opens it sees your name beside your place. The game asks you before it shows your name; until you say yes your place is shown under a handle; you can turn your name off in Settings at any time, and on there whenever the board can be reached. If you play on the Arena ladder, every player who opens the standings of your division or meets your warband there, and, while your warband is among the ten at the top, every player who opens the standings, sees your name, your Syndicate's name and emblem, and what you wear beside your warband; if a device that remembers a child's year of birth has opened your save, and that save has since reached our server with cloud save on, a handle the game makes up stands in place of your name, on every device. Otherwise your name is visible only to you.

Syndicate names, notices and emblems

A syndicate name is visible to other players, and a syndicate notice to its members. Names and notices that are abusive, hateful, sexually explicit, impersonate us or our staff, impersonate another player or syndicate, share links, social media handles, phone numbers or other contact details, or break the law are not permitted. The same rules as for your player name apply.

The game checks names and notices automatically before accepting them. Any player can report a syndicate name from the standings, any player can report a name shown on the Richest Hoards board from the board itself, any player can report a name shown on the Arena standings from the standings, and a member can report a guildmate's name or the notice from the syndicate's own screen, or hide a guildmate on their own device. We may reset a name or remove a notice that breaks these rules.

Invite codes are for inviting players you choose. Posting a code somewhere public means anyone may use it. A code works once and stops working after a week.

A Syndicate's leaders choose whether it is listed, open to anyone, or by request. A member removed from a Syndicate cannot rejoin it from the list for seven days; its leaders may invite them back sooner.

How Syndicates work

The members of a Syndicate held on our server are real players who joined by invite, from the Syndicate list, or by asking its leaders. The opponents it is set against are made by the game: the Feud's rival is generated and matched on war rating, and the Reckoning fills its standings with generated Syndicates when there are too few real ones.

Your save data

Your progress lives on your device. While cloud save is on we keep copies of it on our server so you can recover it on a new device. We will make reasonable efforts to keep those copies safe, but we cannot promise it will never be lost, and we are not liable for lost progress. Deleting your account is permanent.

If two of your devices play on the same cloud save, their copies are combined: the one that has made more progress is kept, and it takes from the other the creatures only that copy owns (with the gear and skins they wear; where both copies own a creature of the same kind, the stronger one is kept and the other becomes shards of that kind), the record of what you have bought, your eggs and wild kin, and lifetime records such as the deepest stage you reached. Anything already claimed or spent today stays claimed or spent. The other copy's currencies (Gems included), the gear in its bag, its current stage and the warband it was fielding are not kept.
