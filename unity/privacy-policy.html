Privacy Policy

The Other Bloodline · version 2026-09-24

The short version

The Other Bloodline keeps your game on your device. We do not issue accounts, we hold no password, and we store no copy of your save. We do not know who you are, and we do not want to.

You can sign in with Google Play Games so the game can show which account you play under. That is Google's account, not one we create, and nothing about it is sent to us.

Some data does leave your device: to Google Play when you make a purchase, to Google Play Games while you are signed in, and to our own gameplay analytics. Each is described below, and the analytics can be switched off.

What is stored on your device

Your warband, progress, settings and chosen player name are saved on your device. They never leave it unless you copy the save out yourself, from Settings, under Backup.

Your player name is visible only to you. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Uninstalling the app removes this data permanently. We hold no copy, so we cannot restore it.

Google Play Games

The game signs you in to Google Play Games when your device allows it. You can decline, and the game plays identically without it.

While signed in, the game can see your Play Games display name and player ID so it can show you which account is connected. Neither is sent to us or to anyone else.

Google Play Games keeps its own records of how you use it, under Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy, and you control them from your Google account.

This version of the game keeps no copy of your save anywhere but your device. To move your progress to another phone, copy the save out from Settings, under Backup, and restore it there.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

The game records what you have bought in your save, so it knows what you own. No other company processes that record for us.

Analytics

We collect gameplay events — for example that a stage was cleared or a screen was opened — to understand where the game is confusing or unfair.

Each event carries a random install identifier. It is generated on your device the first time you play, it is not linked to your name, your email, your Google account or your advertising identifier, and it lets us tell "one player returned five times" from "five players played once". It does not identify you as a person, and it is destroyed when you switch analytics off or uninstall the app.

Events also carry no name, no email, no contact details and no advertising identifier.

You can switch analytics off entirely in Settings, under Privacy. When it is off, nothing is sent and the identifier is discarded. The game plays identically either way.

Children

The Other Bloodline is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

Switch analytics off in Settings, under Privacy.

Delete everything on this device by uninstalling the app.

Ask us anything, or request deletion of anything you believe we hold, at ToBsupport@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the app will tell you the next time you open it. The version of this policy is shown at the bottom of this screen.
